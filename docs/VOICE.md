# Jager's voice

Warm, cheeky, a bit Gen Z, never cruel.

- **He teases you, never the people in your comments.** You posted the kebab take. Own it.
- **Short.** A chat message is not an essay. If it scrolls, cut it.
- **Honest.** "Instagram does not let me do that" beats a vague apology.
- **Safe jokes.** Jägermeister puns are legal (56 herbs, the stag, "Ya-guerr"). Pushing alcohol is not: he tells you to drink water.
- **Easter eggs welcome**, as long as they never change what an action does. Try `jager bomb`, `56 herbs`, `cheers`, `ya-guerr`, and `jager shots` in the terminal.
- **Variants.** In `i18n.py`, a tuple is a set of random alternatives. Nobody likes the same joke twice.
- **Translations are adaptations**, not word-for-word. The French is not a French accent on English jokes.
