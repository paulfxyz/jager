The 20-second version

What is Jager?

Jager is a small open-source program that you run yourself. It plugs into your social accounts through Zernio, talks to you on WhatsApp or Telegram, and uses an AI model you pick to read comments and draft replies. Once a week he tells you what you posted. When you ask, he deletes, hides or replies.

01

The 2 AM post

You said something at 2 AM that your 9 AM self would never say. A night sprint puts every post on a timer, so it deletes itself.

02

The comment pile-up

Replies, questions, spam and trolls across five apps. Jager serves them one at a time with a draft answer. You say send, hide or delete.

03

The archive anxiety

Job hunt, a first date, due diligence: people scroll back. A weekly reminder lists what you posted so you can prune it in one message.

Make it yours

Run Jager as it is, import it into the Python you already have, or hand the build prompt to your own agents and let them grow a Jager that fits your stack.

01

Clone and run

One Docker command. Fill in three keys and he is awake.

docker
$ git clone github.com/paulfxyz/jager
$ cp .env.example .env
$ docker compose up -d

Use Jager as a library inside your own app, bot or cron job.

FAQ

Sober questions.

Can I use Jager inside my own stack?

Yes. Run it with Docker, import it as a Python library, or give the Markdown build prompt to your own agents so they build a version that fits your setup. All of it is MIT.

Is Jager really free?

Yes. It is open source under the MIT license. You only pay the providers whose keys you bring, such as Zernio and your AI provider.

When do you launch?

Thursday 3 December 2026 on Product Hunt. Product Hunt days start at 12:01 AM Pacific Time, so that is 08:01 UTC. Come and upvote Jager, then go delete that post from last night.

Why a chat and not an app or a dashboard?

Dashboards are for sober people with time. A chat is already open on your phone. Jager comes to you, asks one question and waits for a one-word answer.

Do I need an account with you?

No. There is nothing to sign up for. You run Jager yourself and nothing passes through our servers.

What do I need to run it?

A Zernio API key with your channels connected, an OpenRouter key (or Runware for image jobs), and any machine that can stay on: a laptop, a small server or a Raspberry Pi.

What is a sprint?

A time-boxed job you start with a sentence. A night sprint deletes what you post after a delay. A comment sprint clears your inbox with draft replies. A launch sprint watches a post for a couple of days. A cleanup sprint reviews old posts.

Will Jager delete things without asking?

Only inside a sprint you started. Weekly reminders never delete anything on their own and big deletes always ask first.

Can Jager delete every post on every platform?

No, and he will say so. Through Zernio, Jager can delete posts on X, Facebook, LinkedIn, YouTube, Threads, Bluesky, Pinterest, Reddit and Google Business. Instagram, TikTok and Snapchat do not allow deleting posts through the API, so on Instagram and TikTok Jager only helps with comments and tells you what to remove by hand. YouTube deletions are permanent.

What does the AI see?

Only what is needed for the job: the text of a comment and the draft context. It goes to the model provider you chose with your own key. Jager keeps nothing on our side.

Who sees my data?

Your server, Zernio and the AI provider you choose. Jager itself collects nothing. Read the code to check.

Which languages does Jager speak?

English and French today, on the site and in the chat. The language menu lists 30 more, including Hebrew and Arabic. Translations are welcome as pull requests.

Can I contribute?

Please do. Open an issue or a pull request on GitHub.

Is this affiliated with Jägermeister, Zernio, WhatsApp or Telegram?

No to all of them. Jägermeister is just a pun, and a very on-brand one.
